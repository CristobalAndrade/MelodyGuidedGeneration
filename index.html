Melody-Conditioned Generation

Each melody guide is rendered in three target timbres.

These examples demonstrate melody adherence. Each example shows different phonemes, which are resolved by the unconditional model, since our method provides no control over them.

Guitar

These examples demonstrate melody adherence. The onsets sometimes sound as if played with a pick or bent, which is resolved by the unconditional model, as our method provides no control over them.

Piano

These examples demonstrate melody adherence, but with degraded perceptual qualit. Decays lack piano-like characteristics and onsets are underarticulated. We attribute this to distribution mismatch, as the unconditional prior was trained on polyphonic material, making a monophonic melody line out of distribution.

Long Gap Inpainting

A 0.7 s gap (3.00–3.70 s) is removed from the signal and reconstructed. The blind variant inpaints with no melody guidance; the melody-guided variant conditions the fill on the $f_0$ contour of the missing region.
